How it works

From load to ledger in four moves.

Capacity and demand meet in the open.

Carriers publish anchor trips with route, window, and vehicle class. Shippers browse or push loads from ERP. No private spreadsheet. No “network” that only one broker knows.

Quote with math. Book with intent.

Distance- and weight-aware pricing returns a breakdown before confirmation. Booking reserves capacity and starts the payment authorization path—so the truck isn’t chasing a maybe.

Visibility while it still matters.

Once the load starts, drivers share live location. Shippers and ERPs see tracking URLs and status events. Proof of delivery lands before money is treated as final.

Release, capture, pay out.

Ops releases after POD. Shipper capture completes. Carrier earnings hit the ledger on a published schedule—turning settlement from a negotiation into a process.
